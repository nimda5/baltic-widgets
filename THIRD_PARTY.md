# Third-party material and data provenance

The project includes an existing Inbox.lv portal design snapshot, logos, fonts, promotional images and news/weather demonstration material. These assets retain their respective owners' rights; this repository does not grant a new license to them or imply affiliation with Inbox.lv. The snapshot is a UI demonstration, not a live news feed.

Zodiac SVGs: [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide), ISC/MIT; license included in `public/assets/zodiac/LICENSE.txt`. Astronomy: [Astronomy Engine](https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy), MIT. Other npm dependency licenses are distributed with those packages.

Data sources are identified in JSON and parser modules: operator websites, LEA, FuelEst, European Commission Weekly Oil Bulletin, Elering/Nord Pool, EEX NGP, ECB, NBP, Coin Metrics and World Bank Pink Sheet. Preserve source metadata and attribution when reusing data and follow the applicable source terms.

National averages, regional exchange indices and station observations remain separate datasets. Collection timestamps do not replace publication timestamps.

Name-day provenance is preserved per profile in `data/namedays/`. Dictionary classifications and attestation metadata are extracted from the exact national-language entries of [English Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/), attributed to Wiktionary contributors under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); entry URLs and revision IDs are retained. The [VLKK Lithuanian personal-name dataset](https://data.gov.lt/datasets/2664/) supplies official classifications. [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/) structured biographies are CC0. Reviewed name-family references include Behind the Name. Greetings and explicitly labeled vignettes are original editorial content. The calendar was supplied by the user; no new license is asserted for it. See NAMEDAYS.md.
